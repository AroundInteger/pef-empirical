\section{Conclusion}

The paper asked three questions.
What does $\eta$ measure?
Why does that measure diverge from $I(X;Y)$ and from $\Delta\mathrm{ML}$ under the probit model?
Do the rugby and football KPIs, or the three supporting domains, show $\eta$ tracking predictive gain?

The Paired Efficiency Factor $\eta=(1+\kappa)/(1+\kappa-2\sqrt{\kappa}\,\rho)$ compares the variance of the difference with unpaired measurement.
The formula is distribution-free and contains Fisher's equal-variance result as the case $\kappa=1$.
Positive $\rho$ reduces the variance of the difference ($\eta>1$).
Negative $\rho$ inflates it ($\eta<1$).
The cell also records which side is more variable.
Swapping the labels of A and B sends $\kappa$ to $1/\kappa$ and leaves $\eta$ unchanged.
For these leagues, A is home and B is away.

Efficiency is not information about the outcome.
Under the probit model, $I(X;Y)$ depends on the pair only through the relative signal $d_{\mathrm{rel}}$.
At a fixed ratio $\delta/\sigma_{\mathrm{A}}$, a larger $\eta$ raises $d_{\mathrm{rel}}$ and lowers $I(X;Y)$.
Inside the model the relative feature is Bayes-sufficient, so $\Delta\mathrm{ML}$ is at least zero, and the gain is a function of $(d_{\mathrm{rel}},r_{AX})$.
The Fisher case $\eta=1$ still shows a gain.
That is a property of the model.
It is not a feature-choice rule for these KPIs.

On the rugby and football KPIs, the correlation of $\hat\eta$ with $\Delta\mathrm{ML}$ is $\PEFcorrEtaML$, and the correlation of class separation $\hat d_Y$ with $\Delta\mathrm{ML}$ is $\PEFcorrIpredML$.
Four exemplars at comparable class separation do not separate the sign of $\Delta\mathrm{ML}$ from fold noise (\cref{tab:exemplars}).
Rugby and football occupy all four cells and concentrate in Quadrant~3, where pairing is inefficient (\cref{fig:pef_landscape}).
Healthcare, clinical genomics, and finance show the same $(\kappa,\rho)$ geometry, with different mean $\hat\eta$ (\cref{tab:validation}), and they have no match outcome.

Pairing efficiency does not decide whether a team KPI should be relativised.
The predictive choice is a comparison of the absolute feature, the difference, and the pair $(X_{\mathrm{A}},X_{\mathrm{B}})$, reported with an interval.
