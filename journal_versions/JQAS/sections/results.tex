\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}

The probit scenarios show how $I(X;Y)$ and $\Delta\mathrm{ML}$ behave inside the model. The sports landscape shows where the KPIs sit, and that $\hat\eta$ does not track $\Delta\mathrm{ML}$. The supporting domains show the same $(\kappa,\rho)$ geometry outside sport, without a match outcome.

\subsection{Theoretical Foundation: PEF--Information Content}

The theoretical relationship between $\eta$ and information content (\cref{eq:mi_closed}) was examined in four named scenarios from the theory-aligned idealised probit simulation (\cref{fig:si_idealised_stratified}; Supplementary~\cref{sec:si_probit}) under assumptions (A1)--(A2), with $n=5{,}000$ per cell, 50 Monte Carlo trials, and five-fold logistic cross-validation matching the empirical protocol. The simulation's data-generating process, estimands, and parameter-recovery checks are specified in full in Supplementary~\cref{sec:si_probit}.

On the admissible $(\kappa,\rho)$ grid, analytic $\eta$ and exact $I(X;Y)$ from \cref{eq:mi_closed} are negatively correlated within each $\delta/\sigma_{\mathrm{A}}$ slice ($r=-0.87$ across the 16 cells at $\delta/\sigma_{\mathrm{A}}=1$; the other slices agree to two decimals). Pooled across slices the correlation is $r=-0.22$. The four scenarios in \cref{tab:scenarios} span the quadrant space under (A1)--(A2). The two cells with $\eta<1$ still show positive mean ML improvement. That pattern is the Validation~4 floor $\Delta\mathrm{ML}\ge 0$, including the Fisher row at $\eta=1$ ($+7.9\%$). It is a consistency check (\cref{sec:eff_power_theory}).

\subsection{Sports KPI Landscape}

These rugby and football KPIs are the empirical setting of the standing claim. They span all four quadrants (\cref{fig:pef_landscape}; $\PEFtotalStudies{}$ KPI studies, \cref{sec:outcome_defs}). Rugby union ({URC}) contributes $\PEFrugbyNkpis{}$ KPIs and football (English Championship) $\PEFfootNkpis{}$; per-KPI $(\hat\kappa,\hat\rho,\hat\eta)$ estimates and season-to-season positions are in \cref{fig:pef_landscape}, and class separation against $\Delta\mathrm{ML}$ is in Supplementary~\cref{fig:si_ipred_vs_dml}. Domain-level summaries are collected in \cref{tab:validation}: rugby mean $\hat\eta=\PEFrugbyMeanEta$ (95\% CI: $[\PEFrugbyCIlo,\PEFrugbyCIhi]$; $\PEFrugbySuccess\%$ of KPIs with $\hat\eta>1$), football mean $\hat\eta=\PEFfootMeanEta$ (95\% CI: $[\PEFfootCIlo,\PEFfootCIhi]$; $\PEFfootSuccess\%$ with $\hat\eta>1$). The cloud shows the concentration of invasion-game KPIs in Quadrant~3 ($\PEFlandscapeQthreeN{}$ of $\PEFtotalStudies{}$ studies; $\PEFlandscapeQthreePct\%$).

KPIs with positive correlation (for example kicks from hand and carries) sit in Quadrants~1--2. KPIs with negative or near-zero correlation (for example turnovers won and penalties conceded) sit mainly in Quadrants~3--4. By \cref{eq:pef}, $\hat\eta>1$ if and only if $\hat\rho>0$, so that split is the formula, not a separate empirical finding. The association of $\hat\eta$ with $\Delta\mathrm{ML}$ on this cloud is $r=\PEFcorrEtaML$, and the association of $\hat d_Y$ with $\Delta\mathrm{ML}$ is $r=\PEFcorrIpredML$ (Supplementary~\cref{fig:si_ipred_vs_dml}). Point estimates of either sign appear. The fold intervals, not a pooled success rate, are what would identify a sign.

\subsection{Model gain and empirical gain}
\label{sec:eff_power}

Inside the model, a less efficient relative feature can still have $\Delta\mathrm{ML}\ge 0$, because $X$ is Bayes-sufficient. That is not a description of the sports cloud. Three supplementary checks keep the two accounts separate.

\textbf{Idealised simulation under (A1)--(A2).} On the theory-aligned probit grid (Supplementary~\cref{fig:si_idealised_stratified}; $n=5{,}000$, 50 trials, five-fold CV), every Quadrant~4 cell has $\eta<1$ and every one of those 16 cells produced positive mean ML improvement. Validation~4 requires that sign inside the model, for Quadrant~3 cells as well as Quadrant~4. Under the exact integral, $\mathrm{corr}(I,\Delta\mathrm{ML})=0.71$ on the full grid. The negative correlation near $-0.70$ in earlier drafts was $\mathrm{corr}(I,\Delta\mathrm{ML})$ from the discarded discriminant expression, mislabelled as a pooled $\eta$--$I$ correlation. Iso-$\eta$ and iso-$I(X;Y)$ contours diverge once $\delta/\sigma_{\mathrm{A}}$ varies (Supplementary~\cref{fig:si_iso_eta_I}). Parameter recovery is Validation~1 in Supplementary~\cref{sec:si_probit}.

\textbf{Quadrant distribution.} High-volume counts (passes, pressures, duels) are typically anti-correlated across opponents, which is why most of these KPIs lie in Quadrant~3. \Cref{tab:exemplars} illustrates one KPI per quadrant at the median eligible $|\hat d_Y|$. It is not a pooled success rate (Supplementary~\cref{tab:si_quad_landscape}).

\textbf{Quadrant~4 and the probit bound.} Team-stratified bootstrap intervals for tackles (rugby, Q4) lie entirely below $\eta=1$ (Supplementary~\cref{sec:si_qc}). Under (A1)--(A2), $\mathbb{P}(Y=1)=\Phi(d_{\mathrm{rel}}/\sqrt{2})$, so the class prior is not one half unless $d_{\mathrm{rel}}=0$. For the Q4 confirmatory KPI, team-blocked CV accuracies for absolute and relative features are close to each other. The gap from the model's Bayes accuracy is expected: real match outcomes are not generated from a single KPI by the probit link~(A2). The Q4 exemplar interval covers zero (\cref{tab:exemplars}). Inside the model the floor $\Delta\mathrm{ML}\ge 0$ holds when $\eta<1$ (Supplementary~\cref{fig:si_idealised_stratified}). On the sports KPIs that floor is not an identified sign (Supplementary~\cref{fig:si_ipred_vs_dml}).

Lineout throws lost (Quadrant~1) has $\hat\eta=\PEFexQoneEta$ and $\hat d_Y=\PEFexQoneDeltaRatio$. Rucks won is the Quadrant~2 pick ($\hat\kappa=\PEFexQtwoKappa$, $\hat\rho=\PEFexQtwoRho$, $\hat\eta=\PEFexQtwoEta$, $\hat d_Y=\PEFexQtwoDeltaRatio$). Corners (Quadrant~3; $\hat\rho=\PEFexQthreeRho$, $\hat\eta=\PEFexQthreeEta$) has $\Delta\mathrm{ML}=\PEFexQthreeDml\%$ with interval $[\PEFexQthreeDmlLo,\PEFexQthreeDmlHi]$. AUC is $\PEFexQthreeAucAbs$ and $\PEFexQthreeAucRel$. Brier scores are $\PEFexQthreeBrierAbs$ and $\PEFexQthreeBrierRel$. The two-feature accuracy is $\PEFexQthreeAccPair$ against a majority baseline of $\PEFexQthreeAccMaj$. Across all $\PEFqThreeAllN$ Quadrant~3 KPIs, $\PEFqThreeNneg$ have a negative fold-mean $\Delta\mathrm{ML}$, $\PEFqThreeNsigNeg$ have an interval lying entirely below zero, and $\PEFqThreeNsigPos$ have an interval lying entirely above zero. The harmful sign is therefore a property of some point estimates, and it is consistent with fold noise for every Quadrant~3 KPI. Under (A1)--(A2) the idealised grid cannot produce $\Delta\mathrm{ML}<0$ (Supplementary~\cref{sec:si_probit}). Empirical point estimates below zero sit outside that model. They are not, on these intervals, a confirmed quadrant effect.

Fouls (Quadrant~4) has $\hat\kappa=\PEFexQfourKappa$, so the pick is off the $\kappa=1$ boundary, $\hat\rho=\PEFexQfourRho$, and $\hat\eta=\PEFexQfourEta$. Cases with $\hat\eta<1$ and a positive point estimate of $\Delta\mathrm{ML}$ still appear among the wider KPI set (\cref{fig:si_ipred_vs_dml,sec:eff_power}). The exemplar itself does not establish that sign.

\subsection{Supporting Validation: Cross-Domain Results}

The supporting domains ask whether similar $(\kappa,\rho)$ geometry appears beyond sport. They are second-moment checks, not match-outcome replications. \Cref{tab:validation} summarises the sports KPIs and the three supporting domains. They show how empirical $(\kappa,\rho)$ structures propagate into $\hat\eta$ and the proportion of units with $\hat\eta>1$ (\cref{sec:outcome_defs}):
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Healthcare} \parencite{nhanes2017pbxo}: pooled $\eta=\PEFhealthMeanEta$ (bootstrap 95\% CI $[\PEFhealthCIlo,\PEFhealthCIhi]$, standard error $\PEFhealthSE$) across $N=10{,}352$ {NHANES} participants pairing systolic and diastolic blood pressure from the same oscillometric measurement (Quadrant~2). Pulse pressure ($\mathrm{SBP}-\mathrm{DBP}$) is the clinically established relative feature. The one healthcare row in \cref{tab:validation} is that single paired study, not a participant count.
  \item \textbf{Clinical genomics} \parencite{brunner2014earlybreast,geoGSE47462}: mean $\eta=\PEFgenomicsMeanEta$ ({SD} $=\PEFgenomicsSdEta$) across $\PEFgenomicsN$ genes ranked by paired-differential signal in {GSE47462}; $\PEFgenomicsSuccess\%$ of genes yielded $\eta>1$. Because the ranking prefers high paired signal, that percentage is not a random-gene rate (\cref{sec:methods}).
  \item \textbf{Finance}: mean $\eta=\PEFfinanceMeanEta$ ({SD} $=\PEFfinanceSdEta$) across $\PEFfinanceN$ {S\&P}~100 names paired with the {S\&P}~500 (2020--2023 pinned snapshot); $\PEFfinanceSuccess\%$ with $\hat\eta>1$. With the index as A and the constituent as B, the typical cell is Quadrant~1 (\cref{sec:methods}).
\end{itemize}
Summary statistics are collected in \cref{tab:validation}. The sports cloud is \cref{fig:pef_landscape}. The supporting domains are second-moment summaries in the table. They are not overlaid on an information surface, and they are not given per-unit match-outcome plots.
