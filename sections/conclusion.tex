\section{Conclusion}

The paper asked three questions about United Rugby Championship and Championship KPIs.
Are they aligned with home win under $\beta_A+\beta_B=0$?
How large is the team-blocked gap of $D$ relative to $(A,B)$ and to $A$?
Does $\eta$, or the quadrant, sort that gap?

Most KPIs are near the base rate, with $\PEFrugbyNinfo$ rugby and $\PEFfootNinfo$ football KPIs informative under the skill rule.
The median likelihood-ratio statistic is $\PEFrugbyMedLR$ in rugby and $\PEFfootMedLR$ in football, both below $\PEFLRaic$.
The median $D$ versus $(A,B)$ gap matches $-1/(2n_{\mathrm{train}})$.
Within sport, $\eta$ does not sort that gap.
The cell describes the cloud.
It does not choose the feature.
