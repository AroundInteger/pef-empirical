OVERALL ASSESSMENT

IJPAS is the cleanest specialist home after JQAS. Its scope is analysis of actual sports performance, modelling and predicting performance, and technologies for analysis — exactly this paper’s rugby and football KPI spine. The signed Q3 harm result and the four-exemplar table are the kind of finding the journal’s readers can use. The obstacle is length. A typical IJPAS original paper is no more than 7,500 words inclusive of tables, references, figure captions, footnotes and endnotes (official instructions page timed out during this review; this limit is the current secondary-source figure and must be re-checked on the T&F instructions page before upload). Inclusive of captions and a 47-item reference list, the present ~8,000-word body is over that cap before any table text is counted. The Theoretical Framework is also heavier than IJPAS usually prints.

RECOMMENDATION: Reject and resubmit

Rationale: The contribution matches the journal, but the manuscript needs a substantial shortening and a performance-analysis voice, not a statistics-journal structure. That is a rewrite of presentation, not of the study. A cut-down version would likely come back as minor/major revision rather than reject.
