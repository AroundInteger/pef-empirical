OVERALL ASSESSMENT

JSAMS is the venue of the Scott et al. URC KPI paper, so the citation lineage is real. The journal’s current aims and scope, however, are sport and exercise medicine, injury, exercise therapy, physical activity and public health, and “practically/clinically meaningful” sport science (biomechanics, physiology, motor control, psychology, nutrition). This manuscript is a paired-efficiency and match-outcome feature-engineering paper. It does not study injury, clinical outcomes, or an exercise intervention. On top of the scope mismatch, the draft fails JSAMS packaging: ~8,000-word body (cap 5,000), unstructured 337-word abstract (needs a 250-word structured abstract), 9 main display items (cap 6), and 47 main-text references (cap 40).

RECOMMENDATION: Reject

Rationale: As submitted, the paper is out of scope and over every hard JSAMS limit. A cut-down rewrite would still be a performance-analysis / sports-statistics article. That work belongs at JQAS, EJSS, or IJPAS, not at JSAMS under the 2026 guide for authors. This is a venue mismatch, not a judgement that the science is unsound.

MAJOR CONCERNS (must be addressed)

1. Aims and scope (§journal, not §paper)
   Required sub-disciplines: sport and exercise medicine; sport injury; exercise therapy; physical activity and public health; sports science in the biomechanics / physiology / motor-control / psychology / nutrition sense. Authors unsure of fit are told to consult the editorial office first.
   Why it matters: high probability of editorial reject without referees.
   Suggested resolution: do not submit this manuscript to JSAMS unless the editor has confirmed in writing that a match-outcome KPI methods paper is acceptable as “Sport Science.” Even then, JQAS or EJSS is the better use of the same rewrite effort. If you only need the Scott/Bennett visibility, cite JSAMS; do not target it.

2. Structured abstract, 5,000 words, 6 display items, 40 references
   Original research: ≤5,000 words excluding title, abstract, tables/figures, legends, acknowledgements, references; structured abstract (Objectives, Design, Method, Results, Conclusions) ≤250 words; ≤6 tables and figures combined; ≤40 references; up to 6 keywords, at least 4 from MeSH, not repeating the title.
   Current draft: ~8,025 body words; 337-word unstructured abstract; 6 tables + 3 figures; 47 unique main cites.
   Why it matters: any one of these can stop the paper at technical check.
   Suggested resolution: only relevant if an editor has pre-cleared scope. Then: 4,500-word IMRaD; structured abstract; keep `tab:exemplars`, `tab:quadrants`, `tab:validation`, Fig. 1, Fig. 3 (5 items); move the rest to supplementary; cut the reference list to 40 (keep Scott/Bennett, Fisher, Cover, one football-modelling paper; drop finance/education extras).

3. Double-anonymised package and mandatory statements
   Separate title page (word counts, table/figure counts, preprint status, social handle); anonymised manuscript; cover letter naming article type (Original Research) and a sub-discipline; ethics statement with committee name and approval number for “studies in humans”; competing interests; funding; generative-AI declaration if used.
   Sports KPI analysis of professional matches is observational and typically not a Declared human-subjects experiment, but JSAMS still asks authors to state the ethical guidelines followed. NHANES and GEO use is secondary public data.
   Suggested resolution: write an ethics paragraph that distinguishes (i) professional match KPIs under league/club agreement, (ii) public NHANES/GEO, (iii) no new human recruitment. Do not invent a clinical trial registration.

4. “Participants” language and sex-inclusion policy
   JSAMS forbids “subjects” and has a sex-inclusion policy aimed at human participant studies. This paper has teams, not recruited athletes with sex-stratified outcomes.
   Suggested resolution: another sign the journal’s reporting apparatus is built for a different study class.

5. Shared numeric/methods defects
   Healthcare N = 1; unused statistical battery; Q4 recipe vs −0.3% ΔML. These would still need fixing in any rewrite. They are not why JSAMS is the wrong venue.
