OVERALL ASSESSMENT

CSF can host a sports paper — the 2021 Buldú et al. special issue on nonlinear dynamics and networks in sports is the precedent — but only if the manuscript is a complexity / information paper that uses sport as a primary case, not a sports-stats paper with an η appendix. The present draft is the latter: sports title, sports abstract, sports-first contributions, and implicit rather than explicit information-geometry framing. Intellectual depth is not the problem. Against that special issue the PEF draft is stronger on formal theory and cross-domain breadth, and more honest about prediction limits. It is weaker on complexity vocabulary and on putting healthcare, genomics, finance, and manufacturing in the main argument. As submitted, the likely outcome is a desk reject for scope, not a technical reject.

RECOMMENDATION: Reject and resubmit

Rationale: The science can meet the 2021 sports-issue bar, but this is a different paper (title, abstract, opening, and main-text balance), not a cover-letter reframe. CSF also requires Highlights and a ≤250-word abstract that the current 337-word sports abstract does not satisfy.

MAJOR CONCERNS (must be addressed)

1. Scope and opening (§title, abstract, Introduction)
   CSF accepts papers whose primary subject is nonlinear science / complex phenomena. Mathematical papers need a clear link to physical insight or new qualitative features. Interdisciplinary interest and access for a non-specialist audience are explicit. A title about “team KPIs” and “match-outcome prediction” signals sports analytics.
   Why it matters: editors desk-reject out-of-scope submissions without referees.
   Suggested resolution: retitle along the matrix CSF variants, e.g. “Information content and paired efficiency in competitive complex systems.” Open with paired competitive measurements and an efficiency–information trade-off; rugby and football become the high-resolution case, not the only story. Cite Buldú et al., Chaos Solitons Fractals 142 (2021) doi:10.1016/j.chaos.2020.110518, plus Petersen & Penner (metric renormalisation) and one feature-to-outcome football paper from that issue (Li et al. or Medina et al.).

2. Cross-domain evidence must live in the main text
   For CSF the six-domain table is the hook, not SI decoration. Supporting domains currently report η only, and manufacturing pairs (θ, θ̇) are a weaker analogue of home–away KPIs than the prose implies.
   Why it matters: without a real cross-domain argument the paper is “sports with extra datasets.”
   Suggested resolution: one results subsection that places all six domains on the (κ, ρ) plane (Fig. 1 already does this) and states clearly what is and is not replicated (geometry yes; match-outcome ML no). Soften manufacturing and genomics as geometry checks.

3. Highlights and abstract packaging
   CSF: concise factual abstract ≤250 words; 1–7 keywords (avoid multi-word “and/of” strings where possible); Highlights as a separate file, 3–5 bullets, each ≤85 characters including spaces; editable .tex source (PDF is not an acceptable source file).
   Current abstract ~337 words; no highlights file; no keyword list.
   Suggested resolution: 250-word information-first abstract; keywords such as paired measurements; information content; variance ratio; competitive systems; efficiency. Draft Highlights as short claims, for example:
   - PEF maps pairing efficiency from a variance ratio and correlation
   - Efficiency and outcome information can move in opposite directions
   - Invasion-game KPIs sit mostly in the anti-correlated regime
   - Four matched-signal exemplars confirm both help and harm
   (Count characters before upload.)

4. Companion material vs CSF appetite
   CSF is the one empirical venue that can absorb more η-surface / information-geometry language. That still does not mean pasting companion proofs into this paper. One paragraph on the η(κ, ρ) surface and the iso-η vs iso-I tension (already in SI Fig. S2) is enough. Leave ψ-scale theorems in the companion.

5. Shared science defects remain
   Healthcare N = 1; Methods template stats; Q4 recipe. A complexity-science referee will treat N = 1 as a data-handling error.
