## 1. Journal of Quantitative Analysis in Sports (JQAS)

**Publisher:** De Gruyter Brill (ASA official journal)

**OA update (from journal page, accessed 2026-09-19):** from 2026 JQAS transfers to open access year-by-year under Subscribe-to-Open. Articles appear under CC BY 4.0. **No publication costs for authors.** This improves on the July 2026 matrix note (“2024+ JQAS S2O”).

**Manuscript rules**

- 8.5 × 11 in, 1 in margins, 11–12 pt Unicode font, **single column**, **double-spaced** (~26 lines/page)
- Typical length **20–30 pages**; longer discouraged
- Abstract **about 200 words**; **3–6 keywords not in the title**
- References: Harvard on the HTML page; Chicago author-date in the PDF. Treat as **author-date**, not Vancouver numeric
- Figures: separate EPS / TIF / JPG, reproduction-ready; uniform sans-serif lettering ~8 pt; multi-panel A, B, C
- Tables stay in the manuscript
- Unpublished, not under review elsewhere, all authors approve
- Ethical/legal declarations template on the journal page

## 6. JRSS Series C (Applied Statistics) — requirements + fit note

**Publisher:** Oxford University Press

**Portal:** https://mc.manuscriptcentral.com/jrssc  
**Office:** jrsssc.editorialoffice@oup.com  
**Review:** single-blind  
**ORCID:** submitting author required

**Scope:** applications **central**; application usually **in the title** and prominent in abstract, introduction, and discussion. Novel statistical contribution by new/adapted methods or innovative combination of advanced methods. Papers where the application is only an illustration, or that use well-established methods on data, are not suitable. Simulation-only papers are not suitable.

**Length:** longer than **24 journal pages (~12,000 words)** unlikely, except discussion papers. Use supplementary files.

- **Data and code policy:** if accepted, authors must make data and code available, or state why not and how access can be obtained
- Preprints on non-commercial servers allowed
- Colour figures: author-pays in print (£50 per figure) unless policy has moved — confirm at submission
- Optional OUP style / Overleaf template; PDF acceptable at first submission
